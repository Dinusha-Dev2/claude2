Canton Fair Phase 1 — 4 Days at Pazhou Complex
Four full days at Canton Fair Phase 1 — the world's largest trade fair held at the Pazhou Complex in Guangzhou. Phase 1 covers industrial products, electronics, hardware, machinery and building materials. Daily hotel shuttle or metro transfer arranged each morning. Meet factory owners and export representatives face-to-face, negotiate pricing and MOQs, inspect samples and shortlist your best suppliers across four intensive sourcing days.
