The perfect first introduction to Sri Lanka — beaches, whale watching, wildlife safaris, UNESCO wonders, a legendary scenic train and misty tea highlands all in one journey.

3 Beaches + Whale Watch
Beaches & Whale Watching
Golden Beaches & Blue Whale Watching
Begin at Mirissa — Sri Lanka's most beautiful crescent beach — with an early morning blue whale watching tour (best Nov–April). Explore the UNESCO Galle Dutch Fort with its charming cobblestone lanes. Close the journey on Negombo's golden west coast with Dutch canal boat rides just 20 minutes from the airport.
Mirissa Beach Blue Whale Watching UNESCO Galle Fort Negombo West Coast Dutch Canal Boats
Wildlife
Wildlife Safari
Yala & Minneriya Safaris
Spot wild leopards at Yala — the world's highest-density leopard habitat — then witness the extraordinary Minneriya elephant gathering, where hundreds of wild elephants converge around an ancient tank.
Wild Leopards Elephant Gathering Jeep Safaris

Hill Country
Scenic Train & Ella Highlands
Ride the world-famous scenic train through emerald tea valleys to Nuwara Eliya, hike Little Adam's Peak, and photograph the iconic Nine Arches Bridge as trains roll through the misty Ella gorge.
Ella → Nanu Oya Train Nine Arches Bridge Tea Estates

UNESCO Heritage
Sigiriya, Dambulla & Kandy
Scale the 200m Sigiriya Rock Fortress, explore five sacred Dambulla cave shrines with 80 gilded Buddhas, and attend the candlelit puja ceremony at Kandy's UNESCO Sacred Tooth Temple.
Sigiriya 200m Climb Dambulla Caves Kandy Tooth Temple

Ravana Falls & Pinnawala Elephants
Stop at the stunning Ravana Falls cascading through jungle highlands, then visit Pinnawala Elephant Orphanage — over 80 rescued elephants including calves, with the unforgettable Maha Oya river bathing show.
