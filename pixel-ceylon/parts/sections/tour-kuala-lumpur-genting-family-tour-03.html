From the iconic Petronas Twin Towers and Batu Caves to the cool rides of Genting Highlands and a full day at Sunway Lagoon — perfectly crafted for Sri Lankan families with children.

Iconic KL Landmark
City Landmark
Petronas Twin Towers, KLCC Park & Aquaria KLCC
Start Day 2 at the iconic Petronas Twin Towers — once the world's tallest buildings and still KL's most recognisable skyline. Kids run freely in KLCC Park below while you take in the stunning architecture. Then head into Aquaria KLCC for a spectacular 90-metre underwater tunnel experience — sharks, stingrays and thousands of marine creatures surround you on all sides. One of the best indoor family attractions in Southeast Asia.
Petronas Twin Towers KLCC Park Playground Aquaria KLCC 90m Underwater Tunnel Sharks & Stingrays Kids Favourite
1,800m Hill Resort
Hill Resort & Theme Park
Genting Highlands — SkyWay & Skytropolis
Full Day 3 at Malaysia's famous cool hill resort. Take the Awana SkyWay gondola cable car up through the clouds at 1,800m. Explore Skytropolis Indoor Theme Park with rides for all ages, then visit the scenic Chin Swee Caves Temple with panoramic views across the highlands.
Awana SkyWay Cable Car Skytropolis Park Chin Swee Temple
6 Parks in 1
Theme Park
Sunway Lagoon — Water Parks & Rides
Full Day 4 at Sunway Lagoon — Malaysia's top family theme park with 6 parks in one venue: water park, amusement rides, wildlife park, extreme park, scream park and Nickelodeon park. The wave pool and water slides are a massive hit for children of all ages.
Wave Pool Water Slides Wildlife Park
272 Steps
Culture & Nature
Batu Caves & KL Bird Park
Afternoon on Day 2 — climb the spectacular Batu Caves with 272 colourful steps leading to a sacred Hindu temple inside dramatic limestone hills, with friendly monkeys along the way. Then visit KL Bird Park, the world's largest free-flight aviary with flamingos and hornbills.
272 Colourful Steps Limestone Temple KL Bird Park
KL Food & Nights
City Life & Food
Bukit Bintang & Jalan Alor Street Food
Two evenings in Kuala Lumpur's vibrant Bukit Bintang neighbourhood — street shopping, world-class food courts and the famous Jalan Alor open-air food street. Satay, roti canai, nasi lemak and fresh tropical fruit — a lively, family-friendly night out the kids will love.
