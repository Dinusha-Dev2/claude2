Wholesale Market
Yiwu International Trade City — All 5 Districts
Three full days at the world's largest wholesale market — 75,000+ shops across all 5 districts. District 1: toys, jewelry, artificial flowers and accessories. Districts 2 & 3: hardware, electronics, kitchen items and stationery. Districts 4 & 5: garments, bags, bedding and textiles. Flexible MOQs, ready stock and shipping options (FOB/CIF) discussed directly with suppliers. Ideal for Sri Lankan importers looking for variety container loads.

Iconic Shanghai
Shanghai Highlights
The Bund, Yu Garden & Nanjing Road
Walk The Bund's legendary waterfront at night — colonial skyline facing Pudong's futuristic towers across the Huangpu River. Explore Yu Garden and Old Town Bazaar by day, then stroll one of the world's busiest shopping streets on Nanjing Road.

Day Trip
Day Excursion
Zhujiajiao Ancient Water Town
Day trip to Zhujiajiao — a beautifully preserved Ming Dynasty water town on the outskirts of Shanghai. Gondola rides through stone-lined canals, 36 ancient stone bridges, traditional wooden houses and a classic Chinese lunch in the old town.
Ming Dynasty Town Canal Gondola Ride Traditional Lunch

Landmark & Culture
Shanghai Tower & Jade Buddha Temple
Ascend the Shanghai Tower observation deck — at 632m the world's second tallest building — for breathtaking 360° views over the city. Visit the Jade Buddha Temple, an active Qing Dynasty monastery housing two exquisite white jade Buddha statues.
