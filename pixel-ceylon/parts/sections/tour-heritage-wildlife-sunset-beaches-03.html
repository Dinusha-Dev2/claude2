Wildlife & Sunset Beaches

Explore six UNESCO World Heritage Sites, track leopards in Yala, witness the world-famous elephant gathering at Minneriya, ride the most scenic train on earth, and unwind on golden beaches — Sri Lanka's ultimate cultural and wildlife journey in one unforgettable fortnight.

6 UNESCO World Heritage Sites
UNESCO Heritage Trail
Six UNESCO Sites — Ancient Kingdoms & Sacred Temples
This itinerary takes you through six of Sri Lanka's most magnificent UNESCO World Heritage Sites — from the sacred ancient capital of Anuradhapura and the medieval city of Polonnaruwa, to the breathtaking Sigiriya Rock Fortress (200m, 1,200 steps), the Dambulla Cave Temple with its 150+ Buddha statues, Kandy's revered Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic, and the colonial splendour of the Galle Dutch Fort. A true deep-dive into 2,500 years of civilisation.
Anuradhapura Sigiriya Rock Fortress Polonnaruwa Dambulla Cave Temple Temple of the Tooth · Kandy
2 Jeep Safaris
Wildlife & Safaris
Leopard Safari & Elephant Gathering
Jeep safari at Yala National Park — the world's highest leopard density — tracking Sri Lankan leopards, wild elephants, sloth bears and crocodiles. Then experience the legendary Minneriya elephant gathering, where hundreds of wild elephants converge around an ancient reservoir.
Leopard Tracking · Yala Minneriya Elephant Gathering Sloth Bears & Crocodiles
World's Most Scenic Train
Hill Country
Scenic Train & Ella Exploration
Board the iconic train from Nanu Oya to Ella — regularly voted one of the world's most beautiful rail journeys — gliding past emerald tea valleys, misty mountain passes and cascading waterfalls. Then hike Little Adam's Peak for 360° panoramas and photograph the legendary Nine Arches Bridge.
Nanu Oya → Ella Train Nine Arches Bridge Little Adam's Peak Hike
Elephant Experience
Culture & Wildlife
Kandy Culture & Pinnawala Elephants
Witness the moving candlelit evening puja at Kandy's UNESCO Sacred Tooth Temple, take in a Kandyan cultural dance performance, and stroll the Royal Botanical Gardens. Then visit Pinnawala — the world's largest elephant orphanage — for the unforgettable river bathing and feeding experience.
Sacred Tooth Temple Evening Puja Ceremony Pinnawala Elephant Bathing
Golden Beaches & Leisure Days
Beaches & Slow Travel
Sunset Beaches, Ayurveda & Colombo City
Arrive at Negombo's west coast beach with its charming Dutch canals and catamaran sunsets. Unwind in Kandy for a full leisure day of Ayurveda spa, cooking classes and lakeside cafés. Then spend two golden days at Bentota — river boat safari through mangrove forests, water sports, and a romantic beach dinner — before a final Colombo city tour taking in Galle Face Green, Gangaramaya Temple, and the vibrant Pettah Market.
Negombo Beach & Dutch Canals Kandy Ayurveda Leisure Day Bentota River Boat Safari Water Sports Colombo City Tour
